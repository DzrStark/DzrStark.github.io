About me

Zirong Ding丁梓镕

I’m a PhD student at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Haoqian Wang. I earned my B.Eng. in Information Engineering from Xidian University.

My research focuses on robotics and embodied intelligence. I investigate methods for improving vision-language-action (VLA) models and world action models (WAMs), with an emphasis on reliable long-horizon manipulation.
